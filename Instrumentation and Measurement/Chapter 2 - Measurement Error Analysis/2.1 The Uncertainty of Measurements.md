Precision is often reported quantitatively by using relative or fractional uncertainty:

$$
\text{Relative Uncertainity}=|\frac{uncertainity}{\text{measured quantity}}|
$$
$$\text{Relative Error}=\frac{\text{measured value}-\text{expected value}}{\text{expected value}}$$
$$\text{Fractional Uncertainity}=\frac{uncertainity}{average}$$
