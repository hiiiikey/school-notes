# Other 

1. Absolute/Primary Instruments
	- gives the magnitude of quantity under measurement in terms of physical constants of the instrument
	- don't require comparison with any other standard instrument
	- Examples include: Tangent galvanometer, Raleigh current balance, Absolute electrometer
2. Secondary instruements
	- Quantity being measured can only be determined by the output indicated by the instrument
	- They are direct reading instruments.
