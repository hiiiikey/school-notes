# Sensor
- Gives an output that is proportional to the input applied to it
- Usually have a linear relationship
- Not always the case
# Signal Conditioning Element
- a.k.a the Variable Conversion element
- When the output is in an inconvenient format
	- The change in resistance of a strain gauge cannot be directly measured, and thus a deflection type bridge circuit is used to convert it to a suitable voltage.
	- Amplification of a very weak signal such as a biomedical signal

#### NOTE THAT:
By definition, a transducer is a device the converts from one form of energy to another. The term ‘transducer’ is sometimes incorrectly used to mean ‘sensor’.
# Signal Processing Element
- Needed to improve quality of the signal
- One example is filtering a signal that contains noise
- Output could be in a number of formats:
	- Voltage, Current, Frequency, or On/Off Switch
#### NOTE THAT:
- The combination of the sensor, SCE and SPE is called the **transmitter**.
- Use of voltage, current or frequency has implication in terms of the effect of noise
# Signal Transmission 

## Reasons for Transmission
1. Convenience - I want to be warm when reading data
2. Inaccessibility - because it is located in a narrow tunnel if it is located in a high position.
3. Hazardous location - an area that is accessible, but hazardous to humans (like a nuclear plant)

## Methods of Transmission
1. Cable transmission
	- suffers from the problem of losses and attenuation especially over long distances and from electromagnetic interference.
2. Fiber optics
	- Low losses over long distances
	- Large bandwidth
	- Expensive to set up and maintain, both material-wise and labour-wise
3. Wireless transmission
	- More convenient because of no need for cables
	- Obstacles like walls interrupt signals, and also suffers from attenuation
# Display, recording, or analysis
- Final signal gets utilized here
- One of the following happens
	1. Fed into automatic feedback system
	2. D'd, R'd, or A'd
