# 1.3.2 Control
- The thermostat in a refrigerator or geyser determines the temperature of the relevant environment and accordingly switches off or on the cooling or heating mechanism to keep the temperature constant, i.e. to control the temperature.
- Controlling a variable is rather an involved process and is therefore a subject of study by itself.
