- **At low frequencies:**
    
    - The capacitor **acts as an open circuit** (high impedance), **blocking** the signal.
        
    - The signal at **Vout** is close to **0V**.
        
- **At high frequencies:**
    
    - The capacitor **acts as a short circuit** (low impedance), passing the signal through.
        
    - The signal at **Vout** is almost equal to **Vin**.
