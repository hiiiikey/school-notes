- **At low frequencies:**
    
    - The capacitor **acts as an open circuit** (high impedance), allowing voltage to develop across it.
        
    - The signal at **Vout** remains nearly equal to **Vin**.
        
- **At high frequencies:**
    
    - The capacitor **acts as a short circuit** (low impedance), effectively grounding the output.
        
    - The signal at **Vout** is reduced (attenuated).
