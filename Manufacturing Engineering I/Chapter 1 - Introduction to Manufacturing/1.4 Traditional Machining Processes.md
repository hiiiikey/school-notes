- Also known as conventional machining processes
- Machining is an essential process of finishing by which jobs of desired dimensions and surface finish are produced by gradually removing the excess material from the preformed blank with the help of cutting tool(s).
- Performed on metal cutting machines.
- Processes include:
	- Turning
	- Boring
	- Drilling
	- Reaming
	- Threading
	- Milling
	- Shaping
	- Planning
	- Abrasive processes
		- Grinding
		- Ultrasonic machining
		- Lapping
		- Honing
	- Advanced processes
		- Electrical and chemical means of material removal
		- Abrasive jets, water jets, laser beams, electron beams

## Things that can be machined
- Screw threads
- Accurate round holes
- Straight edges and surfaces

## Disadvantages of machining
- Wasteful of material
- Time consuming
