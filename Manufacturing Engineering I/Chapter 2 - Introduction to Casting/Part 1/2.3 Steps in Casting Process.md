# 1. Pattern making
- Modification of the desired shape to include the different needed tolerances: draft, shrinkage, and machining allowances, as well as gates and risers
- Pattern has shape of casting, and is used to make the cavity in the mold
# 2. Core making
- Production of molds and cores, the insertion of cores into the mold, and the closing of the mold
- Cores are typically sand masses that form the internal surfaces of the part, whereas molds form the external surfaces of the part
# 3. Melting
- Melting and pouring of molten metal

# 4. Cooling and Solidification
- Determined by mold material and riser location
- Affects the material properties, and quality of casting

# Common examples for cast parts

- Door handles
- Locks
- Outer casting or housing for motors, pumps, wheels,
- Toys
