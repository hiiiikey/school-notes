1. Flask
	- The frame in which the mold is formed.
2. Pattern
	- Replica of the final object to be made
3. Parting line
	- Dividing line between the two molding flasks that makes up the mold
4. Molding sand
	- Sand that strongly binds without losing its permeability to air
5. Facing sand
	- Small amount of carbonaceous material sprinkled on the inner surface of the mold cavity to give a better surface finish to the castings
6. Core
	- Separate part of the mold, made of sand, which is used to create openings and various shaped cavities in the castings
7. Pouring basin
	- Small funnel shaped cavity at the top of the mold into which the molten material is poured
8. Sprue
	- Passage through which the molten metal, from the pouring basin, reaches the mold cavity.
9. Runner
	- Channel through which the motel metal carried from the sprue to the gate
10. Gate
	- channel through which the molten metal enters the mold cavity
11. Chaplets
	- Chaplets are used to support the cores inside the mold cavities 
12. Riser
	- column of molten metal placed in the mold to feed the castings as it shrinks and solidifies. It is also known as "feed head"
13. Vent
	- Small opening in the mold to facilitate escape of air and gases
