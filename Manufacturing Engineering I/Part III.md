
20.
Given
$D_o=100mm$
$D_d=25mm$

Required
a)$r_e=?$
b)$F=?$
$e_x=?$

$S_y=300Mpa$
$n=0.22$
$A_o=5000mm^2$
$a=0.8$
$b=1.5$

Solution
a) $r_e=A_o/A_f$
$A=\frac{\pi{D^2}}{4}$
$A_o=\frac{\pi{100^2}}{4}=7853.98mm^2$
$A_f=\frac{\pi{25^2}}{4}=490.87mm^2$
$r_e=7853.98/490.87$
$$r_e=16$$
b) $F=\bar{Y_f}\epsilon_x*A_o$
$\bar{Y_f}=\frac{K\epsilon^n}{1+n}$
$\epsilon=\ln{r_e}$
$\epsilon=ln16=2.77$
$\bar{Y_f}=\frac{300*2.77^{0.22}}{1.22}=307.68MPa$
$\epsilon_x=a+b\epsilon$
$\epsilon_x=0.8+1.8(2.77)=5.79$
$F=307.68*5.79*(7853.98/1000^2)$
$$F=13991.61kN$$
