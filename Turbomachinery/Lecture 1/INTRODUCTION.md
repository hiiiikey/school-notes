A Turbomachine is a device that transfers energy between a continuously flowing fluid and continuously rotating blades or impeller by dynamic action

- Turbopumps - fluid pumps that consist of a rotodynamic pump driven by a gas turbine, typically mounted on the same shaft or geared together
- Pumps as Turbines - When a pump spins the other way
## Steam Turbines
### Francis Turbine
- H = Medium to High
- Runner Diameter = 10m, P_max=900MW
- Could be vertical or Horizontal
- Most common type of turbine

### Pelton Turbine/Wheel
- H = 200-1800m (High to Very High)
- Could be Vertical (high output) or Horizontal (low output)
- Runner Diameter = 4m
- 1 -6 jets

### Bulb Turbine
- Horizontal
- H = 1-25 (Very low)
- P < 100 MW
- D < 9m

### Kaplan Turbine
- H = 5-55m
- Vertical
- P = 220 MW
- D = 11m

## Impulse vs Reaction Turbine
### Impulse Turbine
- Fixed nozzle
- steam enters the blade at a very high velocity (supersonic: 1100m/s)
- exit velocity very high, which results into high leaving velocity loss
- **Compounding** - making use of more than one set of nozzles and blades in series so that either the steam pressure or the jet velocity is absorbed by the turbine in stages to limit the exit velocity loss
## Reaction Turbine
- Moving/rotating nozzles
