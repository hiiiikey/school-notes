# Chapter - Hydraulic turbomachines

Incompressible fluid flow is when Mach number is $\leq$ 0.3
where $M=\frac{V_{flow}}{C_{sound}}$

$P_{ideal}=\rho gHQ$
$P_{actual}=\eta_{h}\rho gHQ$

NOTE
Specific speed formula:
$$\text{Specific Speed }(\omega) = \frac{{\omega\sqrt{ \frac{P}{\rho} }}}{(gH)^{5/4}}$$



The above equation imply that Power Output is determined by the head or the volumetric flow rate.

$H_g$ is the difference in water level between the upstream and downstream water level

Effective head $(H_E)$ = $H_g$ - Losses (Penn Stock)

Losses:
- External loss - Caused by frictional losses at the bearing
- Internal loss - Hydraulic losses, Volumetric losses

$H_{net}=H_e - \text{Internal Losses}$

## Example 1

The preliminary design  of a turbine for  anew hydroelectric power scheme has under consideration a vertical shaft francis turbine with a hydraulic power output of 200MW under an effective head of 110m. For this particular design, a specific speed of $\omega_{sp}=0.9$ is selected for optimum efficiency. At the runner inlet the ratio of the absolute velocity to spouting velocity is 0.77, the absolute flow angle is 68 degrees, and the ratio of blade speed to spouting velocity is 0.6583. At runner outlet, the absolute flow is to be without swirl.

Determine:
- $\eta_{h}$
- $\omega$, Do
- Q
- The axial length of the vanes at inlet.
