## First Law of Thermodynamics

$$
\dot{E}_{in} + \dot{E}_{g}=\dot{E}_{out} + \dot{E}_{st}
$$
- When there are no kinetic or potential energies, or work:
	- $\dot{E}_{in}=\sum q_{in}$
	- $\dot{E}_{out}=\sum q_{out}$

$$
\text{Thermal Diffusivity } \alpha = \frac{k}{\rho c_{p}}
$$
## For heat transfer in three dimensions

$$
q''(x)=-k\nabla T
$$

## Thermal energy generation due to an energy source

$$
\dot{E}_{g}=\dot{q}*dV
$$

where:
- $\dot{q}=\text{rate at which energy generated per unit volume } \left( \frac{W}{m^3} \right)$

### Energy Storage Term

- Represents the rate of change of thermal energy stored in the matter in the absence of phase change.
 $$
\rho C_{p} \frac{\partial T}{\partial t}
$$
