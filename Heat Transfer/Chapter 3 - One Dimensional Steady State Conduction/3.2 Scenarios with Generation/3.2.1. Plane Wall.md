# Temperature Profile 

Consider the following plane wall

![[Pasted image 20250413123525.png|250]]

- Heat diffusion equation is $\Large{\frac{d^2T}{dx^2}+\frac{\dot{q}}{k}=0}$
- Solution becomes:
	- $$T(x)=\frac{\dot{q}L^2}{2k}\left( 1-\frac{x^2}{L^2} \right)+\frac{(T_{s,2}-T_{s,1})}{2}\frac{x}{L}+\frac{T_{s,1}+T_{s,2}}{2}$$
		- Profile is parabolic
		- Heat flux is not independent of x

## Symmetrical Distribution

![[Pasted image 20250413125002.png|250]]

- Occurs when $T_{s,1}=T_{s,2}=T_{s}$
- Temperature profile becomes:
  $$T(x)=\frac{\dot{q}L^2}{2k}\left( 1-\frac{x^2}{L^2} \right)+T_{s}$$ 
	- $T_{max}$ occurs at x=0 (midplane)
	- $T_{max}=\text{T at x = 0} = \frac{\dot{q}L^2}{2k}+T_{s}$
- Therefore:
  $$
\frac{T(x)-T_{max}}{T_{s}-T_{max}}=\left( \frac{x}{L} \right)^2  
  $$
