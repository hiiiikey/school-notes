- If thermal energy is generated in the material at the expense of some other energy form, we have a source: $\dot{q}$ is +ve.
- If thermal energy is consumed, we have a **sink**: $\dot{q}$ is -ve

$$
\dot{q}=\frac{\dot{E_{g}}}{V}=\frac{I^2R_{e}}{V}
$$
where:
- I = the current
- $R_e$ = the electrical resistance
- V = the volume
