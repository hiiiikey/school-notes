## Temperature distribution

$$
\frac{1}{r}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r \frac{dT}{dr} \right)+\frac{\dot{q}}{k}=0
$$
General solution: $T(r)=-\frac{\dot{q}}{4k}r^2+C_{1}\ln{r} + C_{2}$

Boundary conditions: $\frac{dT}{dr}\text{ at r=0 is 0}$

$$T(r)=\frac{\dot{q}r_{o}^2}{4k}\left( 1-\frac{r^2}{r_{o}} \right) + T_{s}$$
where $r_{o}$ is the radius at the edge of the pipe ($T_{o}=T_{s}$)

$$
T_{s}=T_{\infty}+\frac{\dot{q}r_{o}}{2h}$$
