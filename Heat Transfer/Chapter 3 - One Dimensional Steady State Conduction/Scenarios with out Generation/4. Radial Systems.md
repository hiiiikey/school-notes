## Temperature Distribution

$$
\frac{1}{r} \frac{d}{dr}\left( r\frac{dT}{dr} \right) = 0
$$
$$
\Large{{q(r)=-kA \frac{dT}{dr}=-{k(2\pi rL)}\frac{dT}{dr}=constant}}
$$
$$
T(r_{1})=T_{s,1}\,  \, T(r_{1})=T_{s,1} \dots\dots\dots\text{Boundary Conditions}
$$
Therefore, the temperature profile, assuming constant k, becomes:

$$
T(r)=\frac{T_{s,1}-T_{s,2}}{\ln{\frac{r_{1}}{r_{2}}}}\ln{\frac{r}{r_{2}}}+T_{s,2}
$$

$$
q_{r}=\frac{T_{\infty,1}-T_{\infty,2}}{R_{total}}
$$
And
$$
R_{tot}=\frac{1}{h_{1}(2\pi r_{1}L)}+\frac{\ln\left( \frac{r_{2}}{r_{1}} \right)}{2\pi kL}+\frac{1}{h_{2}(2\pi r_{2}L)}
$$


## Critical Radius of Insulation

$$
q=\frac{T_{1}-T_{\infty}}{R_{conduction,insulation}+R_{covecntion}}
$$
![[Pasted image 20250412212709.png|300]]

$$
r_{critical,cylinder}=\frac{k}{h}
$$
$$
r_{critical,sphere}=\frac{2k}{h}
$$

# Spherical Coordinates

Here, $A=\pi r^2$
Therefore:
$$
q_{r}=\frac{4\pi k(T_{s,1}-T_{s,2})}{(1-r_{1})-(1-r_{2})}
$$
$$
R_{t,conduction}=\frac{1}{4\pi k}\left( \frac{1}{r_{1}}-\frac{1}{r_{2}} \right)
$$
