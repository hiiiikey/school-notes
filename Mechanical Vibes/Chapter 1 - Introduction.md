# What's vibration?
Oscillatory motion of a particle/rigid body about an equilibrium position in response to disturbance.

# Mechanical Vibartion
Caused by:
- Imbalance
- Misalignment
- Wear and lossness
- External excitation
Desired ones:
- Conveyor
- Ultrasonic vibration cleaning
- Compressor

Undesired ones include:
- Noise
- Wear
- Fatigue
- Destruction

Vibration can:
- Loosen fasteners
- Cause chatter


# Elementary parts of a vibrating system
1. Spring or stiffness element
2. Mass or intertia
3. Damper

# Degree of Freedom
Minimum number of independent coordinates required to determine completely the positions of all parts of a system.

1. Finite number - discrete or lumped parameter systems
2. Infinite number - continuous or distributed systems

The more the DoF, the more accurate the results.

# Simple Harmonic Motion

- Simplest form of periodic motion
- Deterministic
- Pure sinusodial motion
- $x = A\sin(\theta)=A\sin(\omega t)$
- $\dot{x}=\omega A\cos(\omega t)$ and $\dot{}\dot{x}=\omega^{2}A\sin(\omega t)=-\omega^{2}x$
- $T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega}$
