Coloumb's damping works with friction

$F_{f}=\mu N_{f}=\mu W=\mu mg$

Applying newtons law:
WHEN THE X MOVEMENT IS POSITIVE
$m\ddot{x}+kx=-\mu N$
hence, $x(t)=A_{1}\cos \omega_{n}t+A_{2}\sin \omega_{n}t-\frac{\mu N}{k}$

WHEN THE X MOVEMENT IS NEGATIVE
$m\ddot{x}+kx=\mu N$
$x(t)=A_{3}\cos \omega_{n}t+A_{4}\sin \omega_{n}t+\frac{\mu N}{k}$

So, $m\ddot{x}+\mu mg \times signum(\ddot{x})+kx=0$
use initial conditions of $x(t=0)=x_{0}, \dot{x}(t=0)=0$
# Characteristics of a system with coulomb damping
1. EoM is non linear
2. Natural frequency is unaltered
3. System comes to rest after some time
4. Amplitude reduces linearly
5. $X_{m}=X_{m-1}-\frac{4\mu N}{k}$
