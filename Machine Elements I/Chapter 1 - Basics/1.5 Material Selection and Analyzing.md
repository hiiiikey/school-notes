# Factor of safety
- Only a fraction of the ultimate-load capacity of the member is utilized when the allowable load is applied
- The remaining portion of the load-carrying capacity of the member is kept in reserve to assure its safe performance
- $$\text{Factor of Safety}=\frac{\text{Ultimate Load}}{\text{Working Load}}=\frac{\text{Yield Point Stress}}{\text{Working Stress}}$$
- NOTE: The above relations for factor of safety are for static loading.

 - Also:
   $$
  FoS=\frac{\text{Endurance Limit Stress}}{\text{Design or Work Stress}}
   $$
