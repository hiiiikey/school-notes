## Stresses in fillet welds
$$
\tau=\frac{F_{s}}{A}=\frac{F\sin \theta(\cos \theta+\sin \theta)}{hl}
$$
$$
\sigma=\frac{F_{n}}{A}=\frac{F\cos \theta(\cos \theta+\sin \theta)}{hl}
$$
![[Pasted image 20250526021349.png|300]]

$$
\text{Von Misses Stress }\sigma'=\sqrt{ \sigma+3\tau^{2} }
$$
$\sigma'_{max} \text{ occurs at }\theta=62.5\degree$


For static loading: $\large{τ=\frac{F}{(0.707hL)}}$
